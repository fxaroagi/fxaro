# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 29, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect:
- Account information (email, name, password hash)
- Portfolio data (holdings, performance)
- Usage data (queries, signals requested)
- Technical data (IP address, browser type)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Provide and improve the Platform
- Send service updates and notifications
- Generate trading signals
- Analyze usage patterns
- Prevent fraud and abuse

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- Passwords are hashed (SHA-256)
- Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
- Stored in secure data centers
- Access restricted to authorized personnel

## 4. Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request data deletion
- Opt-out of marketing emails
- Export your data

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use:
- Vercel (hosting)
- Stripe (payments)
- SendGrid (email)
- OpenRouter (AI)

These services have their own privacy policies.

## 6. Children's Privacy

FXARO is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
